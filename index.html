Balushahi Recipe

A perfect halwai-style badusha sweet, boasting a beautifully crisp golden exterior with a melt-in-the-mouth, flaky layered core.

Prep Time 20 m

Yield 16 Pcs

Kitchen Tools

  • Wide mixing bowl

Ingredients

For the Pastry Dough

  • All-purpose flour (Maida) 2 cups
  • Baking powder 1 tsp
  • Pure Ghee ½ cup
  • Cold Water ½ cup
  • Salt ½ tsp

Saffron Syrup (Chashni)

  • Granulated Sugar 2 cups
  • Water 1 cup
  • Saffron Strands 6-8
  • Cardamom powder ¼ tsp

Preparation

  1. 1

    Mix flour, salt, baking powder, ghee, and water into a rough mass. Do not knead. Roll and fold 4 times into thirds to lock in flaky layers. Rest for 30 mins.

  2. 2

    Boil sugar, water, and saffron till a light, sticky single-string consistency forms. Stir in lemon juice and cardamom powder; turn off heat.

  3. 3

    Shape into small balls with a deep thumb indent in the center. Fry on a strict low heat until golden and crispy. Soak in warm syrup for 10 mins.

Halwai Secret: Never over-knead the dough, otherwise the signature layers won't form. Frying must be done at a very low temperature so the core cooks completely.
